Wrap-up

Place: where is it?

  • in / inside: in the seat pocket
  • on: on the tray table
  • at: at the door, at row 12
  • under / below: under the seat
  • above: above the seats
  • behind / in front of: behind the galley
  • next to / beside / near / by: beside the window
  • between: between two seats
  • across from: across from the galley

Movement: where is it going?

  • to / toward: to the exit / toward the front
  • into / out of: into the row / out of the row
  • through: through the cabin
  • across / along: across the aisle / along the aisle
  • past / around: past the galley / around the trolley
  • up / down: up the steps / down the aisle
  • from: from the door to seat 22F

Polite crew phrases

  • "May I show you to your seat? It is by the window, on your left."
  • "Let me help you put that in the overhead bin above row 14."
  • "Please wait behind the yellow line."
  • "Could you step out of the row for a moment?"
  • "The first aid kit is in the galley, next to the oven."
